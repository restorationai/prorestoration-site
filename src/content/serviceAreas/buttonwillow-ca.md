## Restoration emergencies common in Buttonwillow

Irrigation canals, drainage ditches, and nearby farmland mean groundwater and runoff behave differently here than in town: a heavy winter storm or an irrigation system malfunction can push water toward a home's foundation faster than it would in a graded subdivision.
