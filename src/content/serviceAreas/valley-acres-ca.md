## Restoration emergencies common in Valley Acres

Valley Acres sits in the Kern River Valley corridor, where hot, dry summers and a long wildfire season trade off with winter storms that can dump a lot of rain in a short window onto ground that hasn't absorbed moisture in months. That combination stresses roofs, flashing, and older irrigation and plumbing lines that weren't built for sudden saturation. Properties near the river and its drainage channels also deal with a higher water table than homes further into town, which means crawlspaces and low-lying slabs can take on moisture even when the roof and walls are intact. Add in the region's seismic activity, which loosens pipe joints and foundation seals over years, and you get a steady mix of plumbing failures, storm intrusion, and the mold growth that follows any of it if drying is delayed.
