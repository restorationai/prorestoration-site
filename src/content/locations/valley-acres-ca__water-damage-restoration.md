## Why Valley Acres Properties See Water Damage Issues

Most homes in this part of Kern County are built on concrete slab foundations rather than raised crawl spaces, which is typical for Central Valley construction from the postwar boom through the 1980s. When a supply line or drain pipe fails under a slab, water doesn't pool visibly the way it would with a basement leak. It travels along the path of least resistance under tile, laminate, or carpet pad, sometimes showing up as a damp spot ten feet from the actual break. Add in the area's expansive clay soil, which swells and shrinks with seasonal moisture changes, and you get slab cracking that opens a path for groundwater to migrate into the structure during the rare but intense rain events the Valley gets in winter. Irrigation infrastructure common to this agricultural region, including older galvanized supply lines, also fails at predictable rates as fittings corrode.
