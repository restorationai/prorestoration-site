## Keene Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most homeowners policies cover sudden and accidental water damage from a burst pipe, though coverage can get complicated if an adjuster suspects the failure was due to long-term neglect rather than a sudden break, which is a common point of scrutiny on older plumbing systems like the kind found throughout this part of Kern County. We document the source, the extent of the water travel, and the condition of the failed pipe section with photos before any repair begins, which gives your carrier a clear record to work from. ## Local note

On Keene properties with well systems, a burst pipe can sometimes trigger the well pump to run continuously trying to maintain pressure in a line that's no longer closed, which drains the pressure tank and can burn out a pump that isn't built to run dry for long stretches.
