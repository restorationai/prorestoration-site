## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Shafter

We start with a camera inspection of the trunk and branch lines so you can see what's actually inside your system before we touch anything, rather than taking our word for it. From there we isolate the system, seal registers, and use negative-pressure extraction to pull debris toward a single collection point instead of pushing it further into the ductwork or back into the living space. Dryer vent cleaning is handled as its own pass, since lint compaction in older Downtown Shafter homes tends to sit at the roof or wall termination rather than near the dryer itself, which is the opposite of what a quick DIY brush job usually catches. Once the system is physically clean, we run an antimicrobial sanitization treatment through the lines when there's evidence of moisture intrusion or rodent activity, which we see more often in homes backing up to open irrigation canals or ag land on the edges of town.
