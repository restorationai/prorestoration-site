## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Stallion Springs

We start by confirming the water source and whether it's touched a septic line or well casing, since that changes category classification and protective equipment before extraction even begins. Standing water comes out with truck-mount or portable extraction units depending on driveway access, then we pull baseboards and, where needed, drywall to expose wall cavities that stay wet long after the surface looks dry. Moisture mapping with penetrating meters tells us how far the water traveled under flooring and insulation, not just where it's visible. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the space and run until materials hit dry-standard readings, not a fixed number of days. Because many homes here are on well water, we also flag any contamination risk to the client immediately if a leak has intersected a water line, rather than treating it as a routine cosmetic dry-out.
