## Who else might I need to call, and when?

Not every water loss requires the same sequence of calls. Here is a quick triage guide:

**Call 911 or your utility company FIRST if:**
- Water is near an electrical panel, outlet, or appliance and you see sparks or smell burning
- A gas line may have been compromised (smell gas, leave immediately, call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from outside)
- The structure appears unsafe to enter

**Call a plumber (or ProRestoration, they have one) if:**
- The source of water is still flowing and you cannot reach the shutoff
- You suspect a slab leak (warm spot on the floor, sudden spike in your water bill, sound of running water with everything off)
- A supply line, toilet, or appliance connection failed

## What happens when you call ProRestoration Services?

When you call (661) 393-9306, you reach a live person around the clock. Here is what to have ready and what happens next:

**What to have ready on the first call:**
- Your address and a brief description of what happened (pipe burst, appliance failure, flooding)
- Whether the water source is still flowing
- Whether there are any electrical or safety concerns
- Your insurance carrier and policy number if you have it handy (not required to start)

**What the crew does on arrival:**

1. **Safety check.** Before equipment goes in, the crew checks for electrical hazards and structural concerns. Slab-on-grade homes common in Bakersfield can hide moisture migration under the concrete, the crew probes with moisture meters and thermal imaging to find it.

2. **Water classification.** The [IICRC S500 standard](https://www.iicrc.org) defines three water categories. Category 1 is clean water (supply line, water heater). Category 2 is gray water (washing machine, dishwasher overflow). Category 3, also called black water, is sewage or floodwater, it requires full PPE and antimicrobial treatment. The category determines the protocol and affects what your insurance covers.

3. **Extraction.** Truck-mounted or portable extractors pull standing water from floors, carpet, and subfloor. In older East Bakersfield and Westchester homes with hardwood or original tile, the crew adjusts technique to preserve materials where possible.

4. **Drying setup.** Commercial LGR dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are placed to create a drying system, not just pointed at wet spots. Bakersfield's dry heat (100-plus degrees in summer) actually helps ambient drying, but the equipment still runs 24/7 until moisture readings hit the IICRC S500 target range. Typically 3 to 5 days for a standard residential loss.

5. **Monitoring.** A technician returns daily to check moisture readings and adjust equipment placement. You get a written log, that log is part of your insurance file.

6. **Scope documentation.** Before any demolition or rebuild begins, ProRestoration produces a written scope of work. Your adjuster gets a copy. Nothing starts without your sign-off.

## What if I'm not sure whether insurance will cover it?

Most standard homeowners policies in California cover sudden, accidental water damage, a burst pipe, a failed appliance supply line, an overflow. They typically exclude gradual leaks, flooding from outside (that requires a separate flood policy), and damage from deferred maintenance. Bakersfield's hard water and aging galvanized pipes in pre-1970 homes can complicate claims if an adjuster argues the pipe was already failing.

ProRestoration documents the loss thoroughly from the moment the crew arrives: photos, moisture readings, equipment logs, and a written scope. That paper trail is what gets claims paid.
